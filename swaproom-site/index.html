FAQ

Does SwapRoom hold my funds?

No. SwapRoom only displays exchange widgets. Your swap is processed directly by the exchange you choose, and coins are sent to your own wallet.

Do I need to create an account?

No sign-up is required for standard swaps on any of the three desks. Some very large or flagged transactions may be reviewed by the exchange under its own policy.

Why are the rates different on each desk?

Each service sources liquidity differently and quotes include network fees. That's why comparing two desks before a big swap is worth it.

How long does a swap take?

Most swaps finish in 5 to 30 minutes, depending on blockchain confirmations. Bitcoin can take longer when the network is busy.

My swap is stuck. Who do I contact?

Contact the support team of the exchange you used, with your transaction ID. Their support links are inside each widget.
